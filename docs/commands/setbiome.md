# `//setbiome`

Change the biome of your selection.

## Usage

```
//setbiome [-p] <biome>
```

## Arguments

| Argument | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `biome` | The name of the biome. See [//biomelist](biomelist.md). |

## Flags

Flags go right after the command, before the arguments.

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `-p` | Change the column you are in, instead of the selection. |

## Examples

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `//setbiome plains` | Turn the selection into plains. |

## Notes

- You may have to rejoin the game to see the change.
