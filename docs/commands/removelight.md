# `//removelight`

Remove the light in your selection.

Without a selection, it works on the area around you.

## Usage

```
//removelight
```

Also typed: `//removelighting`

## Examples

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `//removelight` | Clear the light data. |

## Permission

`worldedit.light.remove`
