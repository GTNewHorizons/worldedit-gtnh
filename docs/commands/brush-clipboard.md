# `//brush clipboard`

Paste your clipboard where you click.

## Usage

```
//brush clipboard [-ap]
```

Also typed: `//brush copy`

## Flags

Flags go right after the command, before the arguments.

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `-a` | Skip air blocks. |
| `-p` | Paste relative to where you stood when you copied, instead of centered on the target. |

## Examples

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `//br clipboard -a` | Stamp the clipboard without air. |
