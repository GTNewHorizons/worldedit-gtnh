# `//fill`

Fill a hole.

Starts at your [placement position](toggleplace.md) and only goes down and sideways.

## Usage

```
//fill <block> <radius> [<depth>]
```

## Arguments

| Argument | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `block` | The [pattern](../patterns.md) to fill with. |
| `radius` | How far to fill sideways. |
| `depth` | How far to fill down. 1 by default. |

## Examples

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `//fill water 10 3` | Fill a pond. |

## Permission

`worldedit.fill`
