# `/mat`

Change the material of the brush in your hand.

## Usage

```
/mat <pattern>
```

Also typed: `/material`

## Arguments

| Argument | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `pattern` | The [pattern](../patterns.md) to paint with. |

## Examples

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/mat stone` | Paint with stone from now on. |
