# `//green`

Turn the dirt around you into grass.

## Usage

```
//green [-f] [<radius>]
```

Also typed: `/green`

## Arguments

| Argument | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `radius` | How far to go. 10 by default. |

## Flags

Flags go right after the command, before the arguments.

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `-f` | Also convert dirt with another data value, such as coarse dirt. |

## Examples

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `//green 20` | Green the area. |
