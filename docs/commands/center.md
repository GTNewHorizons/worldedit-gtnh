# `//center`

Set the blocks at the center of your selection.

Sets 1 to 8 blocks, depending on whether the sizes of the selection are odd or even.

## Usage

```
//center <pattern>
```

Also typed: `//middle`

## Arguments

| Argument | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `pattern` | The [pattern](../patterns.md) to place. |

## Examples

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `//center glowstone` | Mark the center. |

## Permission

`worldedit.region.center`
